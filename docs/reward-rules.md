# MINE Reward Rules

## Mission Flow

1. First entry to MINE:
   - Prompt creator to complete the First-Order Bonus.
   - Extra one-time reward: USD 5.

2. After the first order is completed:
   - When creator enters MINE, show that the first order is completed and the USD 5 reward has been issued.
   - Upgrade the reward task after the creator confirms the modal.
   - New monthly task: reach 15 orders or USD 500 GMV in the current month.
   - One-time cash incentive: USD 30.
   - After creator confirms the modal, MINE displays this monthly task progress.

3. After the monthly boost task is completed:
   - Show an upgrade modal.
   - Upgrade to the blow-up bonus task.
   - New monthly task: reach 50 orders or USD 1,500 GMV in the current month.
   - Additional one-time cash incentive: USD 50.

## Notes

- Monthly task completion can be based on either orders or GMV.
- Monthly rewards are one-time cash incentives, not proportional reward amounts.
- The current implemented MINE flow is stage-based: First-Order Bonus, monthly boost, then blow-up bonus.
